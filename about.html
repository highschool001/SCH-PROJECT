What is VoterEmpower?

The Multi-Channel Voter Empowerment System is a digital platform designed to help Nigerian voters access critical electoral information through their preferred channels - either via the web (smartphone/computer) or USSD (any basic mobile phone).

Our mission is to bridge the digital divide by ensuring that every eligible voter, regardless of their technology access or digital literacy, can easily find their PVC collection center, verify their polling unit, and access real-time queue information.

This system is a solution to the 8.2 million uncollected PVCs problem identified after the 2023 general elections, providing voters with the information they need when they need it.

Our Mission

To empower every Nigerian voter with accessible, real-time electoral information through inclusive technology that works for everyone - from smartphone users to basic phone users.

Our Vision

A Nigeria where no voter is left behind due to lack of information, and where technology serves as a bridge to democratic participation rather than a barrier.
